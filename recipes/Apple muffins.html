Apple muffins

Description

Enjoy our delicious apple muffins that no one will be able to resist. Hidden in the muffins are tangy apple pieces with cinnamon, and they are topped with a flavourful browned butter cream cheese frosting. Toasted almonds on top create the final touch, adding a delicious crunch. Try our easy muffins to treat yourself or impress your guests with a freshly baked batch.

Ingredients

Apple muffins

  • Butter, room temperature 150 g
  • Sugar + extra 2 tbsp 200 ml
  • Eggs 2
  • Wheat flour 300 ml
  • Baking powder 2 tsp
  • Arla Cravendale Semi Skimmed Milk 50 ml
  • Large apple, diced 1
  • Cinnamon 1½ tbsp

Browned butter cream cheese frosting

  • Butter 60 g
  • Fresh cheese, room temperature 150 g

Topping

  • Almonds coarsely chopped 1 handful

Steps

Apple muffins

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (conventional oven). Line a baking tray with 10 muffin cases or grease a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix flour and baking powder. Gradually mix into the batter along with milk.
  4. Toss apples with the extra table spoons of sugar and cinnamon. Gently fold into the batter using a spatula.
  5. Distribute the batter in the muffin cases. Bake in the centre of the oven for 20-25 minutes. Use a skewer to check when they are done. Let cool completely on a wire rack.

Browned butter cream cheese frosting

  1. Melt butter in a saucepan while whisking until it has browned and has a nutty aroma. Transfer to a heatproof bowl. Allow to solidify but not become fridge cold.
  2. Whip browned butter, cream cheese, icing sugar, and vanilla sugar with an electric mixer until creamy. If the frosting appears to curdle, continue whisking until smooth.
  3. Pipe or spread the frosting onto the cooled muffins.

Topping

  • Toast the chopped almonds and allow them to cool. Sprinkle over the frosted muffins.
