Easter cake pops

Description

Our delicious Easter cake pops hold orange zest flavour and have a moist crumb. They are dipped in white chocolate and decorated with sprinkles for a festive treat. Their egg shape makes them perfect for Easter celebrations, and the rich, creamy texture combined with the playful decorations make them a hit with guests of all ages.

Ingredients

Cake

  • Soft butter or spreadable 130 g
  • Sugar 150 g
  • Vanilla sugar 1 tsp
  • Eggs 3
  • Skyr 100 g
  • Unsprayed orange, finely grated zest 1
  • Wheat flour 150 g
  • Baking powder 1 tsp
  • Salt ¼ tsp

Cream

  • Soft butter or spreadable 115 g
  • Icing sugar 130 g
  • Vanilla sugar 1 tsp

Decoration

  • White chocolate 200 g
  • Cake pop sticks
  • Cake sprinkles

Steps

Cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (conventional oven).
  2. Whisk butter, sugar, and vanilla sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, whisking between each one.
  4. Fold skyr and zest into the mixture.
  5. In another bowl, mix flour, baking powder, and salt, then fold it into the batter.
  6. Pour the batter into a greased springform tin (20-22 cm in diameter).
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes, then allow the cake to cool completely.

Cream

  1. Whisk butter, icing sugar, and vanilla sugar until light and fluffy.
  2. Crumble the cooled cake into small pieces in a large bowl. Use an electric mixer to turn it into fine crumbs.
  3. Add the crumbled cake to the cream and mix until it comes together.
  4. Form 20-25 balls, about 30 g each, and let cool for about 30 minutes.
  5. Shape the balls into eggs and let cool for 1 hour.

Decoration

  1. Melt chocolate and pour it into a glass.
  2. Dip the cake pop sticks in the chocolate and immediately insert them halfway into the top of the egg-shaped cake pops.
  3. Dip the cake pops in the chocolate (using the sticks to hold them) and immediately sprinkle with cake sprinkles.
  4. Let them dry, standing upright, on a piece of baking paper.
